# Legal and provenance rules

## Proprietary game data

Do not commit Diablo/Hellfire proprietary assets or extracted content without redistribution rights.

## Third-party open source

Before adapting code, document exact upstream repository, revision and licence. Preserve required notices and attribution.

## Mod-derived content

Gameplay ideas may be independently reimplemented, but authored dialogue, art, maps, code, music and other expressive content must not be copied merely because it appears in a mod.

When uncertain, classify the candidate as `Reference only` until reviewed.
